## One concrete route a new survival player can follow

Use a fresh **normal Overworld** with the mod loaded before the relevant chunks are generated and `academy-worldgen.toml` `genOres=true` (the default). The retained flat visual fixture world is not a substitute for this natural-world prerequisite. Normal survival, no cheats, no operator-granted items/abilities/power, no QA scene commands, and no fixture prerequisite grants.

1. Do ordinary vanilla survival bootstrap: wood, crafting table, wooden/stone tools, cobblestone furnace, fuel and food. Obtain an iron pickaxe. The mod ores required here need a stone or iron pickaxe as listed below; the iron pickaxe covers all four. This route has no ability requirement at this point.
2. Explore and mine the source Academy ores in stone in newly generated Overworld chunks. Ore **anchors** are Y0..59; this is not a negative-Y/deepslate ore system. Gather 18 Constraint Metal Ore blocks, 1 Imag Silicon Ore block, at least 4 Low-purity Imag Crystals, and 8 Resonant Crystals. Constraint and silicon drop their ore block; imag crystal drops the low crystal directly; resonant ore drops the crystal directly. An unenchanted iron pickaxe suffices. Four imag-crystal ore blocks guarantee at least four low crystals (more may drop).
3. Gather the device-only vanilla inputs: 21 raw iron, 1 raw gold, 6 sand, 33 redstone dust, and at least 1 glowstone dust. Iron/raw gold are smelted below. The gold ingot yields 9 nuggets and only 2 are needed. Six glass yield 16 panes and only 4 are needed.
4. For a fully explicit, ordinary glowstone route, obtain a diamond pickaxe (3 diamonds), bucket (3 additional iron ingots), flint and steel (1 additional iron ingot + 1 flint), make/mine 10 obsidian from water and lava sources, build a cornerless Nether portal, ignite it, enter the Nether, and break accessible natural glowstone without Silk Touch. Glowstone drops dust; retain at least one dust. An iron pickaxe costs another 3 iron ingots. Thus this particular vanilla bootstrap adds 7 raw iron beyond the device bill, plus the diamonds/flint/wood/cobblestone/fuel/food and water/lava access. The device bill is not a claim to include all bootstrap tools or travel supplies. Vanilla resource references: `V:recipe/{crafting_table,furnace,iron_pickaxe,diamond_pickaxe,bucket,flint_and_steel}.json`; `V:loot_table/blocks/{iron_ore,gold_ore,diamond_ore,redstone_ore,obsidian,glowstone}.json`; `V:worldgen/biome/nether_wastes.json` contains the `glowstone` and `glowstone_extra` features. Nether structures/trades/witch loot could offer source-neutral vanilla alternatives, but this route does not depend on them.
5. Locate a source-equivalent natural structure chest containing an Electromaster induction factor if choosing Arc Generation, or a Meltdowner factor if choosing Electron Bomb. Search abandoned mineshafts, desert pyramids, jungle temples, stronghold libraries, or dungeons. Each category is a weighted possible outcome, never guaranteed in any particular chest. Carry only the wanted factor among valid category factors at acquisition time. Keep exploring if a chest lacks it. There is no factor crafting recipe. This search is optional if the player accepts the source's random category outcome; it is required for this route to choose Arc/Electron Bomb specifically without changing source behavior.
6. Actually smelt the 18 Constraint Metal Ore →18 Constraint Ingots and 1 Imag Silicon Ore →1 Imag Silicon Ingot, together with the 21 device raw iron →21 iron ingots, raw gold →1 gold ingot, and 6 sand →6 glass. Use furnace fuel and wait for native cooking; these 47 device smelts take 200 ticks each. The seven bootstrap iron smelts, if using the explicit portal route, are extra. Keep ample legitimate fuel; the previous test's recipe assembly omitted fuel and real waits.
7. Craft the exact original intermediates and devices in the order in the recipe ledger below. In particular, make **three empty** low-crystal Energy Units, then consume one in each Energy-conversion Component. No power is needed for these ordinary crafting steps. Two converters go into Solar and one into Portable. Do not charge those three units first: crafting consumes them without a power refund or container remainder.
8. Place the crafted Solar Generator under visible sky in a loaded chunk. Right-click it and put the crafted **empty** Portable Developer into the native recharge slot. In clear daytime it generates 3 IF/tick; rain gives 0.6 IF/tick; at night or with blocked sky it generates zero. Let its actual server ticker charge the item to 10,000 IF, then retrieve it. Empty generator → full portable requires 3,334 clear-day ticks, about 166.7 seconds at 20 TPS; weather, night, preexisting buffer and server TPS can change the wall time. Do not issue time/weather/power commands or freeze daylight for the natural run.
9. Hold the charged Portable Developer in the **main hand** and right-click. In its uncategorized console type `learn` and press Enter. This is the original device's in-game UI command, not a Minecraft cheat/server command. Remain holding the same portable. Initial acquisition completes five stimulations, 130 ticks, spending 3,900 IF. The inventory factor is consumed at completion and selects its category; without a valid factor, source behavior randomly selects one of the four categories. The player becomes Lv1 automatically. No extra separate level-up operation is needed.
10. Reopen/use the developer's skill tree, select Arc Generation (Electromaster) or Electron Bomb (Meltdowner), and confirm learning. Both are original Lv1 roots with no prior-skill/mastery requirements; Portable tier is sufficient. Three stimulations take 78 ticks and 2,340 IF. From an initially full portable, initial acquisition + first-skill learning spends 6,240 IF and leaves 3,760 IF. The original estimate is lower (3,750/2,250 IF) because source execution uses `++tick > TPS`; preserve that source quirk and charge fully rather than changing it.
11. Close the developer. Press N to open the original preset editor; select a row on Preset #1 and assign the newly learned skill. For simple testing, use the R row (slot 2) to avoid a mouse/vanilla-action collision. Close the editor and tap/release V briefly to activate the ability. Press R with no screen open. Ability acquisition clears presets and leaves activation off, so learning alone does not provide an active input binding. N/V are source controls and have no terminal-app prerequisite. If using a different current preset, return to Preset #1 with C or bind the actual selected preset.
12. Aim Arc at a nearby stone block within 6 blocks and cast once: at zero mastery it spends 30 CP/18 overload, gains 0.0018 skill EXP on block hit, and sets a cooldown. Entity hit instead has base 5 damage and 0.0048 EXP, subject to normal damage events. Alternatively cast Electron Bomb at a safe target: at zero mastery it spends 35 CP/16 overload, gains 0.005 EXP on activation, and its original delayed orb fires after 18 ticks within its 15-block aim range. The portable's IF is not consumed by using the skill; CP/overload are separate, naturally regenerating ability state. No ammunition is required for either root skill. Save and exit, then genuinely restart/rejoin and confirm category, Lv1, learned root, earned EXP, binding and finite item IF survive.

## Apparent cycles and later prerequisites

- **Current Charging is not a starter energy source.** It requires learned Arc Generation at0.3 mastery and would be circular as the first power source. The already-implemented original Solar recipe eliminates that cycle without source changes. Do not lower Charging's prerequisite or add power to crafting outputs.
- **Metal Former is not required for this route.** Canonical source has manual wafer, iron-plate, constraint-plate and chip recipes with the yields shown above. The unported Metal Former provides efficient alternative processing plus genuinely necessary coin/needle/silbarn paths. Its absence still blocks those source systems, but does not block the first root skill.
- **Normal/pure crystals are not ore drops and not ordinary crafting upgrades.** They require Imag Fusor. They are not inputs to either starter device when using low-unit recipe18. Do not use energy-unit recipes19/20 for the starter and then declare low-tier crafting impossible.
- **Later crystal route is also acyclic in production.** Original matter-unit recipe17 uses four constraint plates + glass →4 empty matter units. Source `AC:ModuleCrafting.java:205–207` supplies low→normal with3000mB, normal→pure with8000mB. `AC:src/main/java/cn/academy/crafting/PhaseLiquidGenerator.java:31–38` supplies natural Overworld phase lake attempts (0.3 chance/chunk, startY5..34), unlike historical foundation docs that only looked at ACWorldGen. Current `P:fusion/phase/{PhaseLiquidRules,PhaseLiquidGenerator,PhaseLiquidWorldgenConfig}.java`, `P:data/academy/neoforge/biome_modifier/phase_liquid.json` and feature files wire this natural source. `P:fusion/ClassicMatterUnitItem.java:22–35` captures source fluid into units. At binary double1/120 the uninterrupted completion uses121 work ticks,1452IF, not an asserted atomic nominal1440IF. This later route is source-backed but not needed to close the first-root obligation.
- **Normal/Advanced developers are unnecessary for Lv1 roots.** Their real recipes require normal/pure crystals and further infrastructure. Their presence or mature machine scenes must not be used as evidence that a brand-new player earned their first category.
- **No source shortcut is justified by incomplete discovery UX.** Some older docs describe recipes/machines as pending when they are now integrated; tutorial/terminal/recipe-discovery flows remain incomplete. A supplied recipe guide makes manual crafting possible, but this is not full tutorial parity. Update stale status claims only after the owner verifies the current snapshot.
